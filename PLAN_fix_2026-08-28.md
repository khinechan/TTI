## FIX 2 — thumb_check.py: phantom third color
- Mechanism (reproduced, receipts in session): flattened art with
  soft edges puts ink+garment / ink+fill blends inside plum's
  tolerance-40 bucket; over 0.5% of ink they enter the adjacency
  candidate set and produce phantom pairs. Verified: ink+forest ->
  "chocolate ink / plum" WARN x3; ink+burgundy -> "burgundy / plum"
  FAIL 1.03, exit 1.
- Fix: a color is DECLARED only if it appears near-exactly at full
  res (DECLARE_TOLERANCE, strict count) above the share floor.
  Adjacency candidates come from declared colors only, so edge-blend
  pixels are attributed to their neighboring declared colors and a
  legal 2-color design can never report a third. Bucket-map rows for
  undeclared colors carry the caveat (generalizing the outline one).
- Regression tests use the two reproduced fixtures; pre-fix outputs
  recorded in their docstrings.
- Fix 1 coupling: palette_for() takes the garment's ruled outline
  from cc.OUTLINES (skipped when it aliases a palette color, e.g.
  gold on black).
