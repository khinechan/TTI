## Method notes

- Births: 1980-08-27 America/Indiana/Indianapolis (Huntington, IN: 40.8831, -85.4975), 00:00..23:50 every 10 minutes; each A = `daily.build(dict(CH['A'], hm=(h, m)))`. Indianapolis kept standard time all year in 1980, so every step is a real, unique instant.
- Book convention = A's bodies without Moon and without angles (the noon chart's rule). The 12:00 step equals the book's noon chart: yes.
- Family: `pair_core.attraction_family`, 1° orb, ☌ □ ☍. A→B = A's Venus/Mars to B's points (`chart_b=A, chart_a=daily.natB`); B→A = B's Venus/Mars to A's.
- 28.6 Lib = midpoint(B Venus, A Mars) counts as a hot spot only while that ☌ is ≤ 1°; 18.7 Can = midpoint(A Venus, B Moon + 180) only while that ☍ is ≤ 1°. Chemistry at each birth uses only the spots that qualify there.
- Crossing: the 10-minute steps are bisected to the whole minute ("first minute past 1°") and then to the second ("exact").
- Chemistry: P = `pair_core.pair_score(spots, t)` for each of the 365 days 2026-10-20 .. 2027-10-19. v2 headline = local noon America/New_York (new default); v1 headline = 16:00 UTC (old; the cover's numbers). Scale = P / the run's top × 10 (shown rounded half up to 0.1, as `pair_core.timeline` does; every Δ is unrounded); windows = runs of consecutive calendar days with P ≥ 50% of the run's top. Each day's sky longitudes are computed once (a memo around `astro_core.lon`) because the sky does not depend on A's birth time. v1 windows match `pair_core.timeline` on the noon chart: yes.
- max|Δ| = max over the 365 days of |scale(birth) − scale(noon)|, unrounded: (i) each run divided by its own top, (ii) both divided by the noon run's top. Edge shift = the largest move of a window's start or end, in days, when the window count matches noon (n/a otherwise).
- v2 note: local noon is 23 h or 25 h after the previous local noon across a DST change (2026-11-01, 2027-03-14). Windows here join consecutive calendar days. `pair_core.timeline`'s own test `(t − prev).days == 1` would split a window at a 23 h gap; v2 windows that span such a gap (noon chart and all 144 births): none.
- Luck: 己卯 (the pillar after 庚辰) starts on the first day X with (X 12:00 UTC − birth UTC) / 365.2425 d ≥ its start age (from `build`'s lucks). Matches `daily.luck_branch` on the start day and the day before at all 144 steps: yes. Slope = least squares of the exact start instant on birth hour, births before the crossing.
- Day pillar: `bazi_core.day_index(1980, 8, 27, h + m/60)`; the BaZi day turns at 23:00.
- "About" in the briefs is judged as: ±30 min for a time, ±3 days for a date, ±0.5 day/hour for the slope. No number was tuned to match a brief.
